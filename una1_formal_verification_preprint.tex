\begin{abstract}
Large language models frequently hallucinate mathematical proofs because autoregressive token prediction lacks an independent deductive kernel and an explicit epistemic abstention boundary. In this paper, I present a weight-independent formal verification and counterexample discovery architecture that decouples candidate conjecture generation (\texttt{conjecture\_generator}) from a four-engine deterministic verification pipeline (\texttt{Lean 4} dependent-type kernel, \texttt{SymPy} symbolic computer algebra, \texttt{mpmath} 50-decimal-place interval/numerical analysis, and native Python reference verifiers) governed by a fail-closed epistemic abstention gate (\texttt{fail\_closed\_abstention\_gate}). By exploiting the logical asymmetry between universal claims ($\forall x, P(x)$) and existential refutations ($\exists x, \neg P(x)$), the system produces complete, zero-axiom machine-checked disproofs of false conjectures while automatically abstaining from unproved continuum universality claims. I release an open-source benchmark suite (\texttt{Una1Formal}) containing 26 zero-axiom formal theorems verified via a programmatic \texttt{Lean.MetaM} kernel comparator (\texttt{cInfo.type == sInfo.type} and \texttt{Lean.collectAxioms == []}) and validated in \texttt{leanprover/comparator}, alongside three upstream contributions to \texttt{google-deepmind/formal-conjectures} (PR \#7917 formalizing OEIS \texttt{A060003} / Guy Problem A19 on Stern--Goldbach odd composite numbers, PR \#7918 formalizing Erd\H{o}s Problem 301 on unit-fraction sum-free sets, and PR \#7919 updating Erd\H{o}s Problem 670).
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

Current automated theorem-proving and autoformalization pipelines face two structural failure modes when applied to frontier mathematical research:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Specification drift and axiom smuggling:} An automated prover may modify the target theorem statement, introduce unproved auxiliary axioms, or invoke \texttt{sorryAx} while reporting a successful compilation.
    \item \textbf{Conflation of finite evidence with universal ($\forall$) proof:} Numerical or finite-cutoff verification of a partial differential equation or gauge-theoretic lattice model is frequently misinterpreted by language models as a proof of a continuum universal theorem.
\end{enumerate}

To address both failure modes simultaneously, I introduce a weight-independent architecture built on three principles:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Four-Engine Cross-Verification:} Every candidate mathematical claim is evaluated across up to four independent computational substrates: (i) the \texttt{Lean 4} kernel (\texttt{v4.32.2}--\texttt{v4.36.0}), (ii) \texttt{SymPy} symbolic CAS, (iii) \texttt{mpmath} 50-decimal-place arbitrary-precision arithmetic, and (iv) standalone zero-dependency Python reference verifiers.
    \item \textbf{Zero-Trust Kernel Comparator Auditing:} Using \texttt{Lean.MetaM} (\texttt{Una1Formal/ComparatorAudit.lean}) and the Lean FRO's \texttt{leanprover/comparator}, every candidate proof in \texttt{Solution.lean} is checked against an immutable specification in \texttt{Challenge.lean} to enforce exact definitional type equality (\texttt{cInfo.type == sInfo.type}) and zero axiom dependencies (\texttt{Lean.collectAxioms == []}).
    \item \textbf{Fail-Closed Epistemic Abstention (\texttt{fail\_closed\_abstention\_gate}):} When a problem requires an infinite-dimensional continuum limit or an open universal ($\forall$) proof (such as the Clay Millennium Prize problems), the system verifies finite-dimensional sub-lemmas and records an explicit abstention receipt (\texttt{unproved\_millennium\_breakthrough\_claimed = false}) rather than overclaiming a universal proof.
\end{enumerate}

\section{The $\forall$ vs.\ $\exists$ Verification Asymmetry}

A central design principle of the system is the formal asymmetry between universal theorems and existential counterexamples:
\begin{equation}
  \neg \bigl(\forall x \in X,\, P(x)\bigr) \iff \exists x_0 \in X,\, \neg P(x_0).
\end{equation}
Whereas establishing $\forall x \in X, P(x)$ over an infinite domain $X$ requires an analytic or inductive proof across all elements, refuting $\forall x \in X, P(x)$ requires exhibiting a single finite witness $x_0 \in X$ for which $\neg P(x_0)$ is decidably verifiable in the \texttt{Lean 4} kernel with zero axioms (\texttt{SolutionAxioms = []}).

\subsection{Stern--Goldbach Odd Composites and OEIS \texttt{A060003} (PR \#7917)}

In a 1752 letter to Leonhard Euler, Christian Goldbach conjectured that every odd integer $n > 1$ can be expressed in the form
\begin{equation}
  n = p + 2a^2
\end{equation}
for a prime $p$ and an integer $a \ge 0$. In 1854, Moritz Abraham Stern discovered two odd composite exceptions: $n_1 = 5777 = 53 \times 109$ and $n_2 = 5993 = 13 \times 461$. Whether any third odd composite exception exists above $5993$ remains an open problem (OEIS \texttt{A060003} / \texttt{A058320}; Richard K.\ Guy, \emph{Unsolved Problems in Number Theory}, Problem A19; Laurent Hodges, 1993).

In \texttt{Una1Formal} (\texttt{goldbach\_1752\_full\_disproof\_5777} and \texttt{goldbach\_1752\_full\_disproof\_5993}) and upstream in \texttt{google-deepmind/formal-conjectures} (PR \#7917, \texttt{FormalConjectures/OEIS/60003.lean}), the system formalizes:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Decidable kernel-verified proofs (\texttt{decide +kernel}, zero axioms) that $5777$ and $5993$ are odd, composite, and satisfy $\neg \mathrm{IsPrimePlusTwiceSquare}(n)$ across all $0 \le a \le \lfloor\sqrt{n/2}\rfloor$.
    \item The formal disproof theorem \texttt{not\_forall\_odd\_composite\_isPrimePlusTwiceSquare}.
    \item The open research conjecture \texttt{no\_other\_odd\_composite\_stern\_number} (\texttt{@[category research open, question, AMS 11]}).
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Finite Magma Non-Implication Counterexamples (\texttt{equational\_theories})}

Following Terence Tao's \texttt{teorth/equational\_theories} paradigm, the system constructs and certifies three order-3 magma ($M_3 = \{e_0, e_1, e_2\}$) counterexamples with zero axioms:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Left-Stabilizing Idempotent Non-Associative Magma (\texttt{op}):} Proves $\forall x, y \in M_3, x \cdot (x \cdot y) = x \cdot y$ (\texttt{magma\_left\_stabilizes}) and $\forall x \in M_3, x \cdot x = x$ (\texttt{magma\_idempotent}), while refuting associativity at witness $(e_0, e_2, e_1)$ (\texttt{magma\_not\_associative}).
    \item \textbf{Steiner Quasigroup (\texttt{rpsOp}, $x \circ y = (2x+2y) \bmod 3$):} Proves universal idempotence and commutativity (\texttt{rps\_magma\_idempotent\_and\_commutative}) while refuting associativity at $(e_0, e_0, e_1)$ (\texttt{rps\_magma\_not\_associative}).
    \item \textbf{Medial Non-Commutative Magma (\texttt{leftZeroOp}, $x \star y = x$):} Proves the 81-case 4-variable medial identity $(x \star y) \star (z \star w) = (x \star z) \star (y \star w)$ (\texttt{left\_zero\_magma\_medial\_law}) while refuting commutativity at $(e_0, e_1)$ (\texttt{left\_zero\_magma\_not\_commutative}).
\end{enumerate}
