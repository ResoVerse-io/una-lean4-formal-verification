1. The Core Bottleneck This System Solves
Current LLM-based autoformalization pipelines suffer from two structural vulnerabilities:
(a) Conflation of heuristic search with universal (∀) proof, frequently smuggling unproved claims via sorryAx, altered theorem types, or unexamined axiom assumptions.
(b) Neglect of the formal asymmetry between ∀ and ∃: whereas proving a universal Millennium-class theorem requires infinite-dimensional analytic closure, refuting an auxiliary conjecture (∃ x, ¬P(x)) requires only one finite counterexample witness that can be proved with 100% deductive certainty (SolutionAxioms = []) inside the Lean 4 kernel.
